# PerPix office entrance

## Art direction

A private Manhattan office in 1984: walnut, leather, brass, warm lamplight and a cool CRT. A live Persian neon frame above the founder uses the homepage slogan translation keys. The camera moves into the CRT and fades into the ready application. Existing destination routes and authentication remain intact.

The cinematic pacing and restrained luxury were informed by [Cartier Watches & Wonders 2025 on Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025), discovered through the requested Awwwards websites collection. The office artwork and implementation are original.

## Motion and behavior

### Random technology-inspired environments

Each entrance chooses one of three equally likely scenes once on mount: the original Manhattan office, a Turing-inspired computing workshop, or a contemporary Nikola Tesla-inspired electrical laboratory. React state keeps the scene stable while images load and the entrance exits. Only the selected environment and the shared dashboard are requested. Consecutive visits may choose the same scene. No preference storage, additional dependency, or extra minimum loading time is introduced.

The new scenes are imaginative tributes, not documentary reconstructions. Turing's workshop deliberately connects historic computation to a modern PerPix screen; Tesla's laboratory refers to Nikola Tesla's electrical inventions, not the automotive brand. Each scene has its own calibrated monitor mask, camera target, and neon frame palette.

### Shared entrance behavior

Native CSS transform and opacity transitions avoid adding dependencies or loading Three.js for the entrance. The office is a still image with camera motion, lighting and screen treatments, rather than animated character footage. The normal entrance waits at least 1.8 seconds for initial router readiness and observed shell-blocking queries, then uses a 1.2-second exit. The initial user profile is marked `bootBlocking`; cached data and background refetches do not delay entry. The artwork never gates entry. Skip and Escape dismiss even if the initial route is pending. A 10-second watchdog releases the modal when initialization stalls; the router's existing loading or error fallback remains responsible for the pending route. Skip, Escape, timeout, and reduced motion use a short fade without the zoom. The dialog closes before the overlay unmounts so native focus restoration and modal cleanup can complete.

A native modal dialog contains keyboard focus while the entrance is active. Composition is checked at 360 × 640, 375 × 812, 768 × 1024, 1440 × 900 and 812 × 375. Chromium and WebKit tests cover viewport bounds, keyboard exit, reduced motion, failed artwork and revealing the authenticated dashboard. Automated browsers bypass the entrance unless local storage `perpix:boot-splash` opts in with `on`.
