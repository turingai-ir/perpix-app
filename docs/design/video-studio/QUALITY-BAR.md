# Video studio — bounded acceptance card

Recorded at finish against the existing surface; this is not a concept tournament or a new design-system contract.

- Processing is legible at 375px and reports only the actual request phase, without fabricated progress or ETA.
- A stationary, aspect-aware editing frame replaces the logo-centric animation; the single scan can be paused and respects reduced motion and visibility.
- Completed clips fit without cropping, retain native controls, and expose preview/download clearly.
- Media-loading retry refreshes playback without generating another paid video.
- Persian copy feels conversational and lightly playful in creative states; failures give plain recovery instructions.
- RTL, keyboard focus, established tokens and mobile action reflow remain intact.

Evidence: six targeted generation tests and seven output-control tests; inspected idle, processing, decoded-output and dialog captures on desktop and mobile. This acceptance scope excludes live paid generation and unrelated surrounding layout.
